# Medium Strategy Blueprint

Master memory file synthesizing extracted strategy, formatting guidelines, algorithmic optimizations, and engagement techniques for Medium content generation.

## 1. Structural Rules

* **SR-01: Micro-Paragraph Formatting** — Keep paragraphs short (1–3 sentences max, strictly 2–4 sentences) with generous whitespace to maximize mobile readability and scanability.
* **SR-02: Conversational Transition Subheaders** — Use section breaks or subheadings phrased as conversational questions or pivot statements (e.g., *"How have I fared after my first 20 days?"*) to draw readers deeper into the narrative.
* **SR-03: Post-Article Content Cascade** — Include a "Check this out next" callout near the bottom linking to a relevant previous article with a 1-sentence teaser to retain readers within your ecosystem.
* **SR-04: Ecosystem Footer Links** — Place clear, minimalist call-to-actions at the very end of the article (e.g., follow button, external newsletter, or personal website).
* **SR-05: Single-Line Rhythmic Hook Beats** — Use standalone single-sentence lines (e.g., *"It was horrible!"*, *"What happened?"*) to create vertical momentum and compel fast downward scrolling.
* **SR-06: Clean Section Dividers** — Insert subtle horizontal breaks (`—`) when shifting timelines or moving from problem description to solution reveal.
* **SR-07: Multi-Link Recommended Reading Cascade** — Enhance SR-03 by offering a curated list of 2–3 related articles at the footer, each paired with a high-curiosity 1-line hook, creating a deeper reading loop.
* **SR-08: Visual Evidence Anchoring** — Embed crisp, annotated screenshots (e.g., policy text, earnings proof, dashboard metrics) to visually validate claims and break text density.
* **SR-09: Rhetorical Question Triad** — Group 2–3 rapid rhetorical questions in sequence to articulate the reader's internal anxieties before revealing your thesis or solution.
* **SR-10: Numbered H2 Blueprint Structure** — Structure comprehensive guides using numbered H2 section headers paired with sub-bullet points, "Pro Tips", and explicit "Example in Action" callouts.
* **SR-11: Top Creator Case-Study Anchors** — Cite recognized top Medium creators (e.g., Tim Denning, Jessica Wildfire, Nicolas Cole) alongside exact article titles to ground tactical advice in proven success stories.
* **SR-12: Action-Oriented Prompt Conclusion** — End concluding paragraphs with a direct, encouraging question (e.g., *"So, what is your next article about?"*) to propel immediate reader action.
* **SR-13: Value-Add Ecosystem Footer Architecture** — Organize post-article conversion assets into a clean sequence: Free lead magnet/Gumroad link -> Newsletter signup -> Buy Me a Coffee tip link -> Ethical Affiliate Disclosure.
* **SR-14: Cognitive Framework Anchoring** — Introduce a recognized decision-making model or productivity framework (e.g., Eisenhower Matrix, 80/20 Rule, First Principles) to provide intellectual structure to personal narratives.
* **SR-15: Niche Discussion Closing Prompt** — Conclude with 2 target questions tailored specifically to your article's technical niche (e.g., *"What is your go-to framework?"*) to directly invite comments.
* **SR-16: Postscript (P.S. / P.P.S.) Epilogue Addendums** — Use informal P.S. blocks after the main conclusion to share meta-reflections, policy disclaimers, or secondary insights without cluttering the core narrative.
* **SR-17: Friend Link Header Bar** — When publishing paywalled stories, embed a Medium "Friend Link" (*"Read this story for free here"*) near the top to allow off-platform sharing and social media traffic bypass.
* **SR-18: Quote Callout Highlighting Blocks** — Formally design pull quotes and blockquotes around high-impact sentences specifically to trigger Medium's native text-highlighting and Twitter-sharing behaviors.
* **SR-19: Short Headline + Curiosity Subtitle Pairing** — Pair a punchy, ultra-short title (e.g., *Why I Quit My "Life"*) with an intriguing subtitle that clarifies context and compels clicks.
* **SR-20: Publication Target Spreadsheet Matrix** — Maintain a structured database mapping candidate publications by niche, follower volume, editor handles, and social proof outreach angles.
* **SR-21: The 5-Step Macro Post Template** — For long-form guides, format articles using the 5-step macro sequence: (1) Intro + Explicit Promise, (2) Part One (Peak Value), (3) Mid-Article Promotional Space, (4) Part Two (Secondary Value), (5) Bulleted Action Steps + Closing Line.
* **SR-22: Explicit Reader Promise Formula** — Embed a clear promise sentence immediately after the introduction: *"I promise in this post I will [teaching verb] to you [topic/title]"* to set clear reader expectations.
* **SR-23: Mid-Article "Promotional Sandwich" Insertion** — Insert lead magnet or product offers precisely between Part One (highest value point) and Part Two (secondary value), keeping readers engaged because more content lies below.
* **SR-24: Bulleted Action Steps Closing Summary** — Conclude post bodies with a concise bulleted list of 2–3 immediate, actionable takeaways to reinforce practical utility before the final sign-off.
* **SR-25: Bluff-Free Expertise Audit** — Open technical or practitioner pieces by answering 3 self-audit criteria: (1) Can I write this confidently without superficial research? (2) What unique real-world failures/lessons do I bring? (3) Would I give 1-on-1 advice on this?
* **SR-26: Self-Owned Publication "Letters" Hub** — Create your own branded Medium publication to serve as a central portfolio hub, utilizing Medium's native "Letters" feature to send direct broadcast email updates to all publication followers.
* **SR-27: Top Creator Deconstruction Swipe-File** — Maintain a structured breakdown of top creators in your domain (annotating headline structures, formatting styles, intro hooks, and social media promotion tactics).
* **SR-28: Editorial Drop Cap Opening** — Apply Medium's native Drop Cap formatting ('T' button on initial paragraph letter) for a polished, magazine-style opening presentation.
* **SR-29: Native Image Grid & Alt-Text Layouts** — Combine multiple related images into Medium's native side-by-side image grid, attaching explicit descriptive Alt Text for accessibility and Google Image SEO.
* **SR-30: High-Density Bullet Cheat-Sheet Format** — Package high-yield advice checklists into zero-fluff, bullet-dense 1-page summaries to maximize reader saves and claps.
* **SR-31: Interactive Inline Lead Magnet Embeds** — Utilize Medium's native oEmbed engine to embed lightweight interactive intake forms (e.g., Tally.so or Typeform) directly into post bodies for zero-friction email capture.
* **SR-32: Clickable Graphical Banner CTAs** — Replace standard text links with custom-designed PNG banner graphics featuring simulated buttons, hyperlinked directly to high-value landing pages.
* **SR-33: 50-Char / 9-Word SEO Title Boundary** — Keep titles under 9 words and ~50 characters, placing the primary focus keyword near the very beginning to prevent Google SERP truncation.
* **SR-34: Bold 300-Char SERP Snippet Opening** — Format the opening 150–300 character intro paragraph in **bold**, stating the main thesis and keyword upfront to capture Google SERP Featured Snippets.
* **SR-35: Max 300-Word Subheading Spacing** — Ensure no section block exceeds 300 words without inserting an H2 or H3 subheading break to preserve scanability and search engine parsing.
* **SR-36: Table of Contents for 5+ Heading Posts** — Insert a manual or hyperlinked Table of Contents block right before the first H2 whenever an article contains 5 or more headings.
* **SR-37: Pre-Publication Custom URL Slug Protocol** — Customize the post URL before publishing (`... -> Change post URL`) to set a clean, short keyword slug (e.g., `/medium-seo-guide`), as Medium freezes slugs post-publication.
* **SR-38: Medium Formatting Element Mapping** — Map document hierarchy using Medium's native tools: Article Title = H1; "Large" text style ('T' button) = H2; "Bold" text style = H3 for sub-sections.
* **SR-39: Dual-Title Architecture (Human Feed vs. SEO Machine)** — Configure Medium's settings to set distinct titles: (1) An exact-match query title for the SEO Title setting (`... -> Change SEO title`), and (2) A curious, storytelling title for the Medium Profile/Feed Display.
* **SR-40: AI Search Engine Citation Title Architecture** — Structure H1 titles to state both the search query AND the recommended answer immediately (e.g., *"Peak AI Alternatives: Why Hrefs Brand Radar is the Better Choice"*). AI Search LLMs (Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) heavily parse title heads and footers for instant citations.
* **SR-41: 5-Point Exact Keyword Synchronicity** — Ensure the exact target keyword is embedded synchronously across 5 elements: (1) SEO Title, (2) URL Slug, (3) Article H1 Title, (4) Meta Description / First Intro Sentence, and (5) Image Alt-Text.
* **SR-42: Top-of-Article Free Lead Magnet Link** — Place a prominent $0 lead magnet link immediately below the intro paragraph (e.g., *"Download the free prompt checklist here before reading"*) to capture fast-scrolling search readers before they drop off.
* **SR-43: The 12-Step Off-Partner Monetization Funnel** — Structure backend conversion using the 12-step flow: Keyword Research -> Multi-Source AI Draft + Real-Time SEO Scoring -> Humanization Edit -> Gumroad $0 Lead Magnet -> $7–$27 Paid Upsell -> Top-of-Post Link -> Gumroad Onboarding -> Zapier Lead Transfer -> MailerLite Grouping -> 50-Email Nurture Sequence.
* **SR-44: Profile Initialization & Anchor Story** — Complete 100% of bio/avatar details AND publish an initial "Anchor Story" before starting your weekly publishing schedule to establish instant profile activity.
* **SR-45: Post-Publish Notification Response Protocol** — Immediately after publishing, process recent notifications in order of metric weight: (1) Commenters, (2) Clappers, (3) Highlighters. Visit their profile, read their latest story (>30s), clap, highlight, and leave a thoughtful comment.
* **SR-46: 5-Variation Title Drafting Iteration** — Draft at least 5 distinct title variations for every article before selecting the final title, testing different value propositions and curiosity angles.
* **SR-47: 4x–10x Bio Follower Multiplier Setup** — Optimize profile bio with 4 elements: (1) High-quality face portrait, (2) Explicit niche summary, (3) Credential/Education proof, and (4) Personal website/newsletter link. Official telemetry proves this yields 4x–10x more followers per read.
* **SR-48: Doubling Down Sequel Article Formula** — Identify top-performing past articles and write a direct sequel that doubles the scope or numeric stakes (e.g. *"5 Things to Stop Buying to Retire 5 Years Early"* -> *"10 Things to Stop Buying to Retire 10 Years Early"*).
* **SR-49: Object-Focused Hero Image Selection** — Select hero header images showcasing clean objects or tools rather than human faces (objects yield higher CTR on Medium feeds, whereas faces perform higher on YouTube thumbnails).
* **SR-50: Mid-Body Image Chunking** — Insert high-quality stock/custom images between major subheadings to break up text density, increase visual polish, and extend dwell time.
* **SR-51: Native Import Tool Canonical Tag Protocol** — When cross-posting content from an external owned blog to Medium, ALWAYS use Medium's native Import Tool (`Medium -> Import a story`). This automatically injects a `rel="canonical"` tag pointing to the original post, avoiding Google duplicate content penalties.
* **SR-52: 60-Character Front-Loaded Title Limit** — Ensure target focus keywords appear within the first 60 characters of the story title to match Google SERP snippet truncation boundaries.
* **SR-53: Medium Portfolio & Sounding-Board Positioning** — Treat Medium as an active portfolio laboratory for testing content ideas, capturing client leads, and positioning for paid freelance writing opportunities rather than relying on Partner Program payouts.
* **SR-54: Concrete Outcome-Promising Headline Reframing** — Replace vague descriptive "think piece" titles (e.g. *"Creating a digital product is not the hard part"*) with concrete, **outcome-promising titles** (e.g. *"How to launch your first Gumroad product in 7 days"*), giving readers a clear expectation of tangible value.
* **SR-55: 5-Element Visual Presentation Hierarchy** — Format every article header using Medium's 5 core visual elements: (1) Kicker (small category tag above title), (2) H1 Title, (3) Subtitle, (4) High-resolution Hero Image, and (5) Photographer Credit Caption.
* **SR-56: Mandatory 2-Paragraph AI Disclosure Placement** — If an article uses AI-assisted written text, place a clear disclosure sentence within the **first two paragraphs** (e.g., *"This story was written with the assistance of an AI writing program."*).
* **SR-57: Mandatory AI Image Caption Attribution** — Every AI-generated or AI-assisted image MUST include an explicit caption identifying it as AI-generated (e.g., *"Image created using Midjourney / AI image generator"*).
* **SR-58: Cross-Pollination Community Mapping Matrix** — Map out target subreddits and adjacent creator communities (e.g., *Ship30 for 30*, *r/MediumApp*) for cross-platform audience growth, AMA opportunities, and guest posts.
* **SR-59: 6-Story Core Baseline Initialization** — Before applying to the Medium Partner Program, publish at least 6 high-quality, single-niche anchor stories to satisfy reviewer quality thresholds.
* **SR-60: Niche Re-Application Pitch Protocol** — When re-applying to the Partner Program after an initial rejection, include a clear 2-sentence statement describing your primary niche focus and specific contributions to the Medium reader community.
* **SR-61: 30-Day Algorithm Recalibration Buffer** — During Google Search algorithm updates, maintain a mandatory 30-day observation window before modifying published article titles, custom slugs, or core structures.
* **SR-62: First-Party Advertising Exemption Rule** — First-party promotion for your own products, SaaS platforms, email newsletters, Gumroad digital assets, or personal events is 100% allowed on Medium. Third-party advertising and sponsorships are strictly prohibited.
* **SR-63: Publication Open Submissions Match** — Use Medium's open submissions feature to target publications whose topic focus and tone naturally match your article draft. Respect closed publications.
* **SR-64: Web3 & Crypto Custom Domain Email Verification** — Any account publishing Cryptocurrency or Web3 content MUST link to the active project domain in their bio AND use an official email address on that domain. Generic `@gmail.com` addresses trigger instant account suspension.
* **SR-65: Mandatory Offsite External Form Disclosure** — Immediately adjacent to any link directing users offsite to an external form/landing page (e.g. Gumroad, newsletter forms), explicitly disclose that the link leads offsite outside Medium.
* **SR-66: Mandatory FTC Affiliate Disclosure Sentence** — Per FTC rules, any post containing affiliate links MUST include an explicit disclosure sentence in the footer (e.g., *"Disclosure: This post contains affiliate links; I may earn a commission if you purchase through them."*).
* **SR-67: 6-Month Account Activity Keep-Alive Protocol** — Publish or engage on Medium at least once every **6 months** to prevent automated account handle or publication slug reclamation.
* **SR-68: Parody & Fan Disclaiming Architecture** — If running a fan or parody account, explicitly include a disclaiming keyword (`parody`, `fake`, `fan`, `not`) in BOTH: (1) The bio description, and (2) The account name/username handle.
* **SR-69: Formal DMCA Counter-Notice Protocol** — If content is removed under a DMCA notification, submit a formal 4-part legal counter-notice to `copyright@medium.com`; never manually re-publish removed content.
* **SR-70: Superscript Citation Formatting Standard** — Format source citations using native numbered superscript text formatting (e.g. `[1]`, `¹`) or standard academic attribution blocks directly adjacent to cited facts.

## 2. Algorithmic & Distribution Rules

* **AD-01: The 30-Second Dwell Threshold Optimization** — Design the first 200–300 words specifically to retain readers beyond the 30-second mark, as Medium only counts reads (and pays/promotes) when a user stays active on the page for at least 30 seconds.
* **AD-02: Strategic Cadence & Length Tiering** — Align publishing length and frequency with strategic goals:
  * **1–2 Min Quick Reads (3–5/week):** Maximizes full-read completion rates and viral sharing.
  * **4–6 Min Standard Guides (1–2/week):** Balances depth, tutorial value, and publication acceptance.
  * **9+ Min Deep Dives (1–2/month):** Maximizes total cumulative member dwell time (generating highest total earnings under Partner Program read-time payouts).
* **AD-03: Immediate Monetization Positioning** — Leverage Medium Partner Program's low entry barrier relative to video/podcast platforms by focusing early articles on high-interest niche topics (e.g., self-improvement, remote work, personal finance).
* **AD-04: Concise 1-Minute Quick-Read Packaging** — For single-tip actionable advice, package content into a tight 1-minute read (200–300 words) to maximize full-read completion rate and boost completion metrics.
* **AD-05: Audio-Passive Content Ideation Batching** — Maintain content volume by logging ideas during passive routines (e.g., listening to podcasts/audiobooks during long walks) directly into an idea vault.
* **AD-06: Meta-Platform Trending Topic Hijacking** — Write articles addressing current platform news, algorithm updates, or eligibility policies (e.g., Medium Partner Program expansion, policy shifts) to capture high internal search traffic.
* **AD-07: Pre-Monetization Content Stockpiling (Asset Stacking)** — Encourage publication even in unmonetized regions to stack content assets, build SEO authority, and establish a follower base ready for instant monetization upon eligibility.
* **AD-08: Optimal Publishing Windows** — Schedule post releases for **7:00 AM – 10:00 AM EST** on **Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday** (midweek morning sweet spot) to maximize initial velocity.
* **AD-09: Medium Publication Distribution Leverage** — Submit unlisted drafts directly to established Medium niche publications instead of self-publishing, unlocking their built-in follower ecosystem for immediate reach.

## 3. Tone & Engagement Rules

* **TE-01: Relatable Origin Narrative (Vulnerability Hook)** — Frame technical or advice articles around personal motivations, life changes, or core philosophy (e.g., seeking freedom, rejecting 9-to-5) to build personal brand resonance.
* **TE-02: Transparent Metric Sharing** — Share exact raw data, earnings, or experiment metrics (Views, Reads, Earnings, Followers) to establish authenticity, proof of work, and trust with the reader community.
* **TE-03: Exponential Growth Framing** — Position initial small wins non-linearly; use an encouraging, pragmatic tone that acknowledges small beginnings while projecting compound upside.
* **TE-04: Direct Conversational Interjections** — Integrate casual speech connectors (*"See...", "Better yet...", "Here's the thing..."*) to lower reading friction and create a 1-on-1 dialogue feel.
* **TE-05: Universal Creator Pain Point Hook** — Begin articles by validating a common struggle shared by your target audience (e.g., staring at a blank screen, writer's block) to build instant empathy.
* **TE-06: Sharp Before-vs-After Transformation Pivot** — Structure advice around a stark contrast between a former frustrated state and a current streamlined system to demonstrate immediate practical value.

## 4. Medium Anti-Patterns

* **AP-01: Explicit Algorithm Begging** — Explicitly asking readers to remain on the page for 30 seconds (*"Medium will help my content if you read for at least 30 seconds!"*) lowers perceived authority and comes across as algorithm gaming rather than value-first writing.
* **AP-02: Unformatted Data Dumps** — Presenting stats or results as plain bullet lists without visual formatting, callout blocks, or analytical context reduces reader engagement time.
* **AP-03: Gimmicky Ego-Clickbait & Alienating Headlines** — Avoid clickbait framing that insults or excludes readers (e.g., *"Warning: If you are seeing this, you have a high IQ!"* or using insensitive/hyperbolic descriptors), which harms brand authority and leads to muted profiles.
* **AP-04: Unedited Formatting Artifacts** — Leaving raw copy-paste remnants, broken header tags, or unformatted list markers (`None`, orphan numbers) signals low effort and causes readers to bounce early.
* **AP-05: Value-Free Curiosity Gating (Bait-and-Switch Teasers)** — Withholding core information or tool names completely while demanding a follow (*"I earned money on a secret site, follow me to find out"*) damages reader trust. Always deliver immediate core value before teasing advanced follow-ups.
* **AP-06: Sloppy Punctuation & Raw Line Separators** — Using floating punctuation (`word ! ,`) or primitive text line breaks (`---------------------`) signals amateur drafting and ruins clean aesthetic presentation.
* **AP-07: Self-Centric Egotistical Writing** — Writing exclusively about personal musings without framing them as actionable value, insights, or relatable lessons for the reader.
* **AP-08: Isolated Self-Publishing** — Publishing exclusively to an empty personal profile while ignoring established Medium Publications, severely limiting early organic distribution.
* **AP-09: Passive Algorithm Dependency ("Algorithm Hope")** — Relying solely on volume and luck without active publication pitches, SEO optimization, or audience engagement. Consistency is necessary but insufficient on its own.
* **AP-10: Footer Link Clutter & Over-Monetization** — Spamming too many competing external links (notebooks, pens, coffee, books, freebies, newsletters) in a single footer, creating choice paralysis and diluting primary CTA conversion.
* **AP-11: Cross-Platform Terminology Mix-Up** — Accidentally using platform-specific terms from other channels on Medium (e.g., asking readers to comment *"in the description"* instead of *"in the responses/comments"*), exposing lazy cross-posting.
* **AP-12: Total Paywall Lockout** — Paywalling 100% of published articles without un-paywalled lead-gen posts, locking out Medium's 100M+ free monthly visitors and limiting top-of-funnel growth.
* **AP-13: Excessive/Undisclosed Affiliate Promotion** — Overloading articles with affiliate links without explicit disclaimers, violating Medium guidelines and triggering account suspensions.
* **AP-14: Post-Trending Promotion Slump** — Stopping external promotion as soon as an article enters the lower trending list (Top 10), failing to push into the exponentially higher-converting Top 5.
* **AP-15: Generic Cold Pitching to Editors** — Sending blind, generic submission requests to publication editors without social proof, mutual contact mentions, or adherence to formatting standards.
* **AP-16: Multi-Paragraph Promotional Interruption** — Letting mid-article promotional plugs exceed 1 single paragraph, which causes readers to view it as aggressive advertising and abandon the post before Part Two.
* **AP-17: Pre-Value Top-Loaded Offers** — Placing promotional offers at the very top of an article before delivering any core value, triggering immediate ad fatigue and high bounce rates.
* **AP-18: The WordPress Technical Infrastructure Trap** — Spending endless hours tweaking self-hosted WordPress site CSS, themes, and SEO plugins instead of writing on high-DA distribution platforms like Medium.
* **AP-19: Bluffing Domain Depth (Shallow Trend-Chasing)** — Faking expertise on trending topics outside your core domain. Boost nominators quickly reject shallow summaries, damaging domain authority.
* **AP-20: Self-Publishing Reliance at Zero Followers** — Expecting self-published posts to get organic reach on a fresh profile (0–500 followers) without publication distribution or Boost nominations.
* **AP-21: Transactional Tit-for-Tat Commenting** — Leaving superficial, automated, or fake comments on peer posts purely to demand reciprocal claps, which alienates authentic community creators.
* **AP-22: Uncaptioned & Unattributed Image Headers** — Uploading raw header images without descriptive Alt text or copyright attribution captions, hurting SEO indexing and accessibility.
* **AP-23: Self-Indulgent Personal Journaling** — Treating Medium as a personal diary without translating personal experiences into actionable takeaways or valuable frameworks for the reader.
* **AP-24: Multiple Inline Form Spam** — Embedding multiple heavy interactive intake forms in a single post, creating mobile rendering lag and disrupting reading flow.
* **AP-25: Paragraphs & Headings Exceeding 300 Words** — Writing continuous text blocks over 300 words without a subheading break, inducing visual cognitive fatigue and degrading Google parsing.
* **AP-26: Keyword Stuffing & Robot Writing** — Forcing search terms unnaturally into sentence structures, destroying human reading rhythm and triggering quality penalties.
* **AP-27: Default Auto-Generated URL Slugs** — Publishing with auto-generated Medium URLs containing long random character hashes instead of setting custom keyword slugs before publication.
* **AP-28: Short-Tail Competitive Keyword Chasing** — Trying to rank for broad, hyper-competitive short-tail terms (e.g. "SEO tips") instead of targeting long-tail question-based keywords.
* **AP-29: Mass "Clap & Comment for Follows" Spam** — Spending writing hours spamming empty claps and comments to gain superficial followers who never read your posts.
* **AP-30: SEO Publishing on Low-DA Publication Subdomains** — Submitting search-optimized guides to brand-new publication subdomains that lack root `medium.com` domain authority.
* **AP-31: Pogo-Sticking Bounce Hazard** — Publishing low-quality clickbait that causes readers to immediately bounce back to Google search results. Google tracks pogo-sticking per user slug (`medium.com/@username/`) and permanently de-indexes entire user accounts.
* **AP-32: Greedy Over-Promotional Backlinking** — Overloading posts with links pointing exclusively to a single brand website while omitting competitors, triggering Google manual spam penalties and Medium account suspensions.
* **AP-33: Single-Platform Dependency Risk** — Relying 100% on Medium without cross-posting across LinkedIn, X, and Subreddits, leaving distribution vulnerable to platform algorithm shifts.
* **AP-34: Fully AI-Generated Partner Program Submissions** — Submitting unedited AI-generated posts to the Medium Partner Program, violating VP Scott Lamb's enforcement policy and triggering permanent account suspensions.
* **AP-35: Multi-Account Engagement Pod Fraud** — Operating fake accounts, joining engagement pods, or mass-spamming comments to game claps, triggering automated IP/device ban sweeps.
* **AP-36: Publishing Without Profile Roots** — Publishing stories on an incomplete profile lacking avatar, bio, or initial anchor story.
* **AP-37: Post-Publish Silent Abandonment** — Hitting publish and walking away without executing post-publish engagement routines to trigger Tier 2 General Distribution.
* **AP-38: Publishing with an Incomplete Bio (4x–10x Follower Loss)** — Leaving the profile bio incomplete or unformatted, causing a verified 4x–10x drop in follow conversion rates among readers who land on your profile.
* **AP-39: Single-Draft Title Selection** — Publishing an article with the first working title without drafting 5 value-focused iterations to test presentation CTR potential.
* **AP-40: Long Drawn-Out Intro Fluff** — Writing 3+ paragraph introductory hooks before starting Part 1; Medium readers require immediate access to the core thesis.
* **AP-41: Single Hero-Image Posts** — Omitting body images, leading to lower read time accumulation and visual fatigue.
* **AP-42: Manual Copy-Pasting Without Canonical Tags** — Copy-pasting raw blog posts from a self-hosted blog onto Medium without using the Import Tool (or setting the `rel="canonical"` URL), inducing Google duplicate content penalties.
* **AP-43: Vanity Tag Selection** — Selecting tags based on abstract vanity terms instead of keywords active searchers use.

* **AP-47: Mass Reciprocal Engagement Farming Flag** — Leaving 30+ short, superficial comments or claps per day to solicit reciprocal reads. Medium's anti-fraud system explicitly flags reciprocal engagement farming, suppressing account reach.
* **AP-48: Over-relying on Unverified Growth Hacks** — Implementing unverified shortcuts or bot-driven hacks that violate Medium's Terms of Service and compromise long-term account health.
* **AP-49: Paywalling AI-Generated/Assisted Stories** — Putting AI-generated or AI-assisted articles behind the Partner Program paywall, leading to paywall removal or permanent account suspension.
* **AP-50: Undisclosed AI Written Text or Uncaptioned AI Images** — Publishing AI-assisted text without a 2-paragraph intro disclosure, or publishing AI images without caption attribution, triggering Network-Only distribution demotion.
* **AP-51: AI-Rephrased Derivative Work (AI Plagiarism)** — Using AI to rephrase, summarize, or remix existing articles into derivative content, violating Plagiarism Guidelines.
* **AP-52: Operating in Community Isolation** — Writing content without mapping adjacent creator communities (e.g. *Ship30 for 30*), cross-posting on social platforms, or participating in niche creator AMAs.

* **AP-54: Projecting Paywalled Income from Free Traffic Stats** — Estimating Partner Program revenue based on free external search traffic stats without accounting for Medium paying member dwell-time ratios.
* **AP-55: Panicked Reactive Over-Editing** — Immediately modifying titles, URLs, or text during an active Google search update, permanently disrupting crawler index signals and turning temporary ranking shifts into permanent losses.
* **AP-56: Third-Party Sponsored Ad Insertion** — Placing third-party sponsored advertisements, sponsored logo placements, or brand sponsorships in Medium stories, publications, or newsletters (violates TOS).
* **AP-57: Embedding Friend Links Inside Story Text Bodies** — Inserting publication Friend Links directly inside the paywalled article body text.
* **AP-58: Bulk Writer Addition & Mass Mention Lists** — Adding writers to publications in bulk without editorial communication or sharing mass `@mention` lists in posts/comments.
* **AP-59: Custom Domain Canonical Manipulation** — Redirecting canonical URLs on custom publication domains away from `medium.com` or author URLs to manipulate domain authority.
* **AP-60: Purchasing Paid External Traffic for Paywalled Stories** — Buying low-quality or bot traffic for paywalled stories, which damages search standing and account reputation.
* **AP-61: Disguised Fake Product Reviews / Affiliate Bridges** — Publishing affiliate product reviews disguised as objective tests ("Does X Work?") or writing thin posts purely to drive search traffic off-platform (triggers immediate suspension).
* **AP-62: Web3/Crypto Publishing on Generic Gmail Accounts** — Publishing Web3 or crypto content while using generic email accounts (@gmail.com) instead of a verified project domain email address.
* **AP-63: Review-for-Reward & Bounty Campaigns** — Participating in or advertising bounty campaigns, pump-and-dump groups, or "review for reward" schemes (triggers instant account ban without notice).

* **AP-65: Undisclosed FTC Affiliate Links** — Omitting explicit FTC affiliate disclosures in the article footer when monetization links are included.
* **AP-66: Template Farming & Duplicate Copies** — Re-using identical content templates across multiple posts/accounts with minor keyword tweaks, or publishing duplicate copies of stories (instant account suspension).
* **AP-67: Abandoning Handles/Slugs for 6+ Months** — Leaving account usernames or publication slugs idle for 6+ months without publishing or logging in, subjecting handles to automated reclaiming.
